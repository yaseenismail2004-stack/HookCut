# Clip scoring

Scores are predictions from transcript evidence, not platform analytics or guarantees. The final Estimated Viral Potential score is calculated from hook strength (20%), predicted retention (20%), standalone clarity (12%), usefulness or entertainment (12%), emotional impact (10%), share potential (8%), save potential (5%), comment potential (5%), loop potential (5%), and visual suitability (3%).

Hook analysis assesses immediate clarity, curiosity, emotion, specificity, audience relevance, scroll-stopping strength, and opening pacing. Greetings, filler, delayed value, unsupported clickbait, incomplete context, and coarse segment timing reduce confidence. Retention scores evaluate the first one, three, and five seconds, continuity, gaps, payoff timing, repeated ideas, and ending completeness.

The UI labels this as `Estimated Viral Potential` / `إمكانية الانتشار المقدّرة`; it never claims guaranteed views or virality.
